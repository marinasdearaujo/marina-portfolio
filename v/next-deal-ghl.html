02 · Speed to lead

A positive reply lands at 2:00 PM

2:00
Instantly marks it Interested and a webhook sends the lead, the reply and the campaign ID to GHL. Contact updated, opportunity opened in Positive Reply, rep assigned.
2:01
The rep gets a push and an email with the reply text, the deal details and one-tap call. The lead gets a short answer in the same thread with the calendar link.
2:05
The rep calls. Booking form captures address, loan type and amount. Stage moves to Call Booked, confirmation and reminders go out.
4:00
Not booked yet? A second email goes out and the call task stays open. Texts only for people who opt in on the booking form, never the cold list.
03 · Nothing goes cold

Two follow-up flows

No-show

  • 0 MINReschedule email with the calendar link
  • SAME DAYCall task for the rep
  • DAY 1Short check-in email (plus text if opted in)
  • DAY 3Email with the deal angle
  • DAY 7Last email, then nurture

Booking again stops the flow automatically.

Missing documents

  • DAY 1Email listing only what is still missing, with upload link
  • DAY 2Reminder
  • DAY 3Call task for the rep
  • DAY 4Reminder
  • DAY 7Final reminder and flag

Checklist fields: ID, entity docs, bank statements, purchase contract, rehab budget. Any stage idle for 5+ days alerts the rep.
