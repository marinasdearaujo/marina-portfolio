02 · Speed to lead

A lead lands at 2:00 PM

The first five minutes decide most of what happens next, so the system does the busywork and the rep only has to call.

2:00
The lead arrives from a Meta instant form, a landing page or a positive email reply (by webhook). Contact created or updated, tagged with the form and ad, opportunity opened, rep assigned (round robin if there's more than one).
2:01
The rep gets an alert with what the lead answered and a one-tap call. The lead gets a short, personal-sounding email with the calendar link, plus a text if they opted in.
2:05
The rep calls. If they book, the stage moves to Call Booked and the show-up sequence starts.
4:00
Not booked yet? A second message goes out and the call task stays open. Next morning, the task comes back to the top of the rep's list.
Leads ready soon go first. One form question ("when do you plan to start?") sorts the queue, so the rep calls the "within 3 months" people before anyone else.

A drip that reacts to what people do

A starting cadence, adjusted once the data comes in. Each email does one job, and the branches make sure nobody gets the same email twice.

DAY 10

DAY 14
Last email, then nurtureA low-key note; after that, one useful email a month.

Opens lie, clicks don't

Apple Mail and some other inboxes "open" every email automatically for privacy, so many people who never read it count as openers. I branch on opens, but a click or a reply is the signal I trust.

Rules on every workflow

  • RE-ENTRYOff, so a contact can't get the sequence twice
  • GOALReply, book or buy pulls them out at once
  • EXIT TAGMarks who finished, for the next campaign
  • HOURSSends inside business hours, in their time zone
04 · Getting people to show up

From booked to on the call

When under half of booked calls show, the problem is usually the days between the booking and the call, not the ads.

After booking

  • NOWConfirmation with the time in their zone and what the call covers
  • DAY -1Something short to watch or read, so they arrive invested
  • 24 HReminder by email, plus text if opted in
  • 1 HReminder with the link

Keep the booking window short. Calls booked more than two days out lose people.

No-show

  • 0 MINReschedule email with the calendar link
  • SAME DAYCall task for the rep
  • DAY 1Short check-in (plus text if opted in)
  • DAY 3Email with a new angle
  • DAY 7Last email, then nurture

Booking again stops the flow automatically.

Waiting on the client

  • DAY 1Email listing only what is still missing, with the upload or payment link
  • DAY 2Reminder
  • DAY 3Call task for the rep
  • DAY 4Reminder
  • DAY 7Final reminder, and the deal is flagged

Any stage idle for 5+ days alerts the rep.

05 · Data back to the ads

Meta learns from the CRM, not from the form

Optimizing for raw leads teaches Meta to find people who fill in forms. Sending pipeline stages back teaches it to find people who book and buy.

Lead with its ad and form→Stage changes in GHL→Event sent to Meta with the lead ID→Campaign optimizes for qualified leads

Which event to optimize for

Booked calls are usually too few for Meta to learn from; it needs around 50 events a week. So I send Qualified as the event the campaign optimizes for, and Booked and Won as the ones we report on.

Before trusting any number

I check match quality in Events Manager and compare the counts with the pipeline. If they don't match, the report waits until they do.

07 · Before anything goes live

What I test, every time

Each of these broke on a real account once. Now they're on the launch checklist.

  • A fake lead through the whole pathFrom the ad form to the rep's alert, the emails and the pipeline, before a cent is spent.
  • Phone numbers with the country codeWithout it, the rep's dialer can't call anyone, and nothing shows an error.
  • The Meta connection, weeklyWhen the connection token expires, leads stop arriving quietly. A weekly check and a test lead after any change.
  • Every branch, with test contactsOne that opens, one that doesn't, one that books: each should end in the right place, with the right tags.
