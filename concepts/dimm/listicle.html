6 reasons light sleepers are switching to contoured sleep masks

If you wake at dawn, on planes, or every time your partner checks their phone, a flat mask was never going to fix it. Here's what's different.

Updated this week · Sponsored by Dimm
1

It seals out light without pressing on your eyes

Flat masks block light by pushing against your eyelids. Contoured masks have molded cups that sit around your eyes, so the edges can seal against your face with zero pressure on the eyes themselves.

Most "light leaks" happen at the nose bridge. The Dimm Contour adds a soft, flexible nose flap for exactly that gap.

2

You can blink, so your eyes stay relaxed

It sounds trivial until you've tried it. With space inside the cups, your lashes don't brush fabric, there's no smudged mascara, and you won't wake up with that pressed-eye feeling.

3

Dawn stops being your alarm clock

In a European summer, the sky starts getting light before 5am. Your body treats light as a wake-up signal, even through closed eyelids. Real darkness lets you sleep until your actual alarm.
